05 · Financial

The money side of parenting, planned properly.

From the first Aadhaar application to your child's first mutual fund, here's what to set up and when.

Government schemes

Built for Indian families

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Long-term, tax-free savings account you can open in your child's name once they're a minor account holder.

Tax-free 15-yr lock-in

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana

For a girl child under 10 — one of the highest guaranteed interest rates among small savings schemes.

Girl child High interest

Janani Suraksha Yojana

Cash assistance for institutional delivery, aimed at reducing maternal and infant mortality.

Maternity Cash benefit
Insurance hub

Cover the family, not just yourself

Maternity health cover

Check your existing policy's maternity waiting period — most require 2–4 years unless added early.

Newborn add-on

Most insurers let you add a newborn to a family floater within 30–90 days of birth.

Term life for parents

A new dependant is the most common reason to review or increase term cover.

Investing for baby

Start small, start early

Child-focused mutual funds

SIPs designed around a 15–18 year horizon, usually with a lock-in until the child turns 18 or 21.

Recurring deposits

Lower risk, fixed returns — a good place to park short-term goals like the first year of school.

Gold savings

Digital gold or sovereign gold bonds as a smaller, traditional allocation many Indian families still prefer.

Tax planning

Deductions new parents miss

Section 80C

PPF, Sukanya Samriddhi and child tuition fees (up to 2 children) all count toward your 80C limit.

Medical insurance premium

Premiums for a family floater covering your child are deductible under Section 80D.

Employer benefits

Check if your employer offers a crèche allowance or maternity/paternity benefit that needs declaring.

Legal & documentation

The paperwork checklist

Birth certificate

Usually issued by the hospital or municipal office within 21 days of birth.

Aadhaar enrolment

Can be done any time after birth; linked to a parent's Aadhaar initially.

Passport & nominee updates

Add your child as a nominee on insurance and investments, and update your own will if you have one.
